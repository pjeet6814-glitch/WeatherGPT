Official alerts

Warnings issued by IMD and state authorities, shown exactly as published. Each one lists when it stops being valid.

How to read an alert

IMD uses four colours in its bulletins. The alerts above carry IMD's own wording, so read the text and the valid-until time first.

  • Green: no warningNo action needed.
  • Yellow: be awareWeather may turn bad. Keep checking for updates.
  • Orange: be preparedSevere weather is likely and may disrupt travel or services.
  • Red: take actionDangerous weather is expected. Follow instructions from your authorities.

"No alerts" means nothing is currently published for your state. It does not mean the weather is harmless. A short-range alert can appear at any time.
