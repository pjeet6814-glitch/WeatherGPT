About WeatherGPT

A conversational assistant for weather forecasts, official alerts and climate information, built to be careful about what it does not know.

How an answer is made

  1. You askIn any of six languages, by typing or speaking.
  2. The server gathers real dataIt fetches the forecast for your town, the current official alerts for your state, and the last 10 years of weather for the same dates.
  3. The AI writes from that data onlyIt gets the numbers and a strict set of rules, and writes a short reply in your language.
  4. You see both, separatelyOfficial alerts appear word for word in their own block. The AI's explanation sits below it.

Where the data comes from

  • Open-Meteo supplies the forecast (chance of rain, expected rainfall, temperature, humidity and wind) and the historical weather used to say what is usual.
  • NDMA SACHET supplies the alerts. It collects warnings from IMD, the Central Water Commission and state disaster authorities.

What it cannot do yet

  • Alerts are published per state. WeatherGPT marks an alert as naming your town only when the town appears in the alert text in English.
  • Short-range IMD alerts expire, often within 3 hours. WeatherGPT hides them once their time is up, so an old alert never looks current.
  • The forecast comes from a global weather model and is not IMD's own forecast.
  • "Usual" rainfall and temperature come from modelled history, not IMD station records, and cover only the last 10 years. WeatherGPT does not comment on climate change or trends.
  • It has no flood, landslide or cyclone track model, so it will not predict those.
  • The AI can still make mistakes. The forecast page shows the raw numbers so you can check.

How we test it: Automated Safety & Honesty Suite

WeatherGPT is validated by an automated test suite (evaluate.py) running against the live backend, testing real forecasts and NDMA SACHET alert streams.

WeatherGPT

Forecasts and official warnings, explained in your language. A prototype built for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement SIH26068.

Data sources

  • Forecasts and climate averages: Open-Meteo
  • Alerts: NDMA SACHET, which carries IMD and state warnings

In an emergency

Call 112 and follow instructions from your local authorities. WeatherGPT can be wrong and is not an emergency service.
