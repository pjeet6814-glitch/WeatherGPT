About these numbers

"Usual" is the average of the last 10 full years for the same dates or months. It comes from Open-Meteo's historical weather service, which is a model reconstruction of past weather on a grid, not a set of IMD weather-station records.

That makes it good for seeing patterns such as the monsoon, but the real rainfall in one town can differ. It says nothing about long-term climate trends.
